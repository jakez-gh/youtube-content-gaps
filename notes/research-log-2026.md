## Hypothesis test areas (real gaps to validate)

1. AI prompt engineering for non-developers
   - Keywords: "prompt engineering Notion", "prompt chain ecommerce", "LLM plugin sales script".
   - Gap signal: many creators show basic prompts but few practical templates for hyper-specific use cases (product pages, marketing, sales call formats).

2. Gig + Web3 tax guidance
   - Keywords: "crypto staking taxes 2026", "gig economy taxes self-employed", "NFT income reporting".
   - Gap signal: urgent regulatory updates every year; many videos are out of date by tax season.

3. Mobile-first creator workflows
   - Keywords: "phone B-roll 15 sec", "AI shorts workflow 2026", "free phone editing YouTube".
   - Gap signal: TikTok/shorts demand continues; few detailed tutorials for budget creators with no desktop.

4. Career pivot + micro-SaaS package
   - Keywords: "no code SaaS launch 30 days", "AI freelancer onboarding", "midcareer switch content strategy".
   - Gap signal: demand from 30-45 professionals; content typically high-level, not playbook.
